The administrator: preparing the ground

  1. Accounts⚙ Admin → Comptes → Règles d'accès: one line per person, with their role, study profile and rights per Space. Dry run first, then “Appliquer”.
  2. The project's Space⚙ Admin → Spaces: the key (e.g. EQP) becomes the ticket prefix (EQP-1, EQP-2…), never reused.
  3. ApproversEveryone chooses their approver (the instructor, the team lead) in the menu under their name → Paramètres; the administrator corrects it if needed (“Feuille de temps…”).
  4. Backups⚙ Admin → Update et sauvegardes: a restore point is taken every day and before every bulk operation.

Start a project with an AI assistant

No need to know in advance which requirements, standards or tests fit your product: an AI assistant suggests them in an interview, one question at a time, and gives you a file to import.

  1. Copy the prompt⚙ Admin → Spaces → Commencer → "Copy the English prompt" (or open starter.md and copy all the text).
  2. The interviewPaste it into a new conversation (Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot). About 26 questions to click (you only type the product and the names of the team), 30 to 45 minutes: the product, energy, radio, hazards, market, team, calendar, then the needs, requirements, tests, the functional tree and all of Diamond, and the pages it suggests and you correct. "I don't know" is a good answer.
  3. The fileSave the .json file it gives (Notepad → Save as, UTF-8 encoding).
  4. The importCommencer → drop the file, read the preview, "Create the Space". Standards arrive "To verify": confirm them one by one before approving a requirement.

The team lead: launching the project

  1. Needs (Goldmine)Start from the needs of the customer and the stakeholders; the generic catalogue of a consumer product (deploy/requis_generiques.csv) imports in one click, then adapts to the CdCF (functional specification).
  2. The plan (Exploration)The phases of the PDP, one MVP loop per phase, the reviews (Lean Canvas, Concept Review, PDR / CDR, Validation Report) and the Gantt; Excel round trip possible.
  3. Sprints (Agile)Backlog → Sprint → Board; each ticket “implements” a requirement.
  4. The decision (Tools)Brainwriting ideation, Pugh matrix, Kepner-Tregoe; the name and folder of every document with Generator.

The course's PDP in the tool

PDP = product development process. Each phase is a Build → Measure → Learn loop (Lean Startup): an MVP tests the riskiest hypotheses, Agile Sprints pace the hardware and software work, and the phase closes on a review and the decision to persevere or pivot. The loops rise like a tornado, wider and faster, up to the launch.

PhaseMVPTime codesDeliverables (Generator)
A. DiscoveryMVP 0: is the problem real?A-01 to A-06Lean Canvas and hypotheses (LCV), market study, preliminary specifications, schedule, risks
B. ConceptMVP 1: do users like the concept?B-07 to B-12Concept Review (CRV), needs, functional analysis, CdCF, QFD, morphological matrix, Pugh

D. Validation et lancementMVP 3: ready for the market?D-FAB, D-19 to D-21Validation Report (VVR), manufacturing, test protocol and results

A funded project: justify every hour

  1. The Funding SpaceTimesheet → ⚙ Admin → Spaces et organismes: create a Funding Space for the programme, with its Budget Lines (claimable or not), then attach the project's Space, its organism and its contract number.
  2. Time entryThe team enters its hours on these Budget Lines, with a short description per range; vacation (INT-VAC) and health leave (INT-SAN) from the INT Space are never claimed.
  3. The claimExcel export (summary, Budget Lines with every range and its justification, detail) or a Word report for the recipient, with the attestation to sign: no retyping.
